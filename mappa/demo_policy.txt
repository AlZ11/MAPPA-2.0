Pulsewise Privacy Policy (fictional app, written for the MAPPA demo)

Pulsewise Pty Ltd ("Pulsewise", "we", "us") makes the Pulsewise heart-health app. This policy explains how we handle your personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and date of birth. When you use the app we collect heart rate readings, blood pressure entries and step counts that you record or sync from a connected wearable. We collect your approximate location, based on your IP address, to show you local clinic information. We do not collect your precise GPS location. We do not access your contacts or your photos.

We automatically collect your device's advertising ID and crash logs so that we can fix bugs and measure how the app is used.

2. Who we share it with

We share your email address with Mailchimp to send you appointment reminders and newsletters, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We share crash logs and your advertising ID with Google Firebase for analytics. We may disclose your health readings to your treating doctor, but only if you ask us to in the app. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we never share your health readings with advertisers.

3. AI features and automated decisions

Pulsewise uses artificial intelligence to write plain-language summaries of your weekly readings. To do this, we send your de-identified readings to OpenAI, which processes them on our behalf. OpenAI does not use your data to train its models. The app also uses an automated risk score, calculated from your blood pressure and heart rate readings, to decide whether to recommend that you see a doctor urgently; a Pulsewise clinician reviews every urgent recommendation before it is sent to you.

4. How we protect and keep your information

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest on servers located in Sydney, Australia. Access to health readings is limited to staff who need it for their role. We keep your health readings for 24 months after your last login, after which they are permanently deleted. If you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days.

5. Children

Pulsewise is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16, and if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing privacy@pulsewise.example. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

7. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm to you, we will notify you and the OAIC as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. Our Privacy Officer can be contacted at privacy@pulsewise.example.
